## Hard rules (override generic skill advice)

1. **Proof.** Follow `intel/case-studies.md` §0 exactly, including its naming rules. No client pricing in public material. Never fill a `[Gap]` by inventing a result or metric.
2. **Voice.** Founder-to-founder. Short declarative lines. Lead with what you remove ("No migration. No new platform."). No corporate speak. Where a skill's copy advice conflicts with `playbook/voice.md`, voice.md wins.
3. **Process.** Outreach, discovery, proposals, pricing, and closing follow `playbook/operating-playbook.md`. Use skills like `cold-email`, `sales-enablement`, `offers`, and `pricing` to sharpen within that process, not to replace it. If a skill suggests a better step, flag it as a playbook change. Don't silently diverge.
4. **Drafts before sending.** Any external message (email, LinkedIn, post) is shown to Aarya as a draft before it goes out.
5. **Channel reality.** Primary lever is outbound (LinkedIn + email). There is no paid ads budget and no self-serve signup funnel, so treat `ads`, `paywalls`, `signup`, `aso`, `sms`, and `churn-prevention` (SaaS sense) as low-relevance unless asked.
